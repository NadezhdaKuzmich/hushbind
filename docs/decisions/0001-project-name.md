# 0001. Project name: Hushbind

- **Status:** accepted, 2026-11-11 (replaces the short-lived choice “Hushknot”)
- **Meaning:** _hush_ (a secret, “shh”) + _bind_ (to tie two people together; in IT, to bind a key
  to a device). A quiet secret that binds a pair.

## Why

- Works beyond family: friends, couples, colleagues, clients.
- `hushbind.com` and `hushbind.app` were unregistered (RDAP, 2026-11-11).
- Deep search (stores, GitHub, companies): no product named Hushbind.
- Short, two syllables, reads one way only (“hush-bind”).

## Rejected

- Taken in our field or confusing: Family code, Tessera, Psst, Codeknot, 2FAmily, Dovira, Rune,
  Sigil, Skytale, Talisman, Wolfproof, Hushword, Symbolon, Unclone.
- Hushknot: `hushknot.com` registered by a third party on 2026-09-16 — a phishing risk for an
  anti-scam product.
- Free runners-up: Hushtether, Hushtie, Hushvow, Bindword, Voxsigil, Clonebane.
